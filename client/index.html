Link

A clean, secure, and modern local file sharing system.
Link allows you to instantly share files, photos, videos, and text across devices on the same local network or via secure private virtual rooms. Operating directly on WebRTC Peer-to-Peer technology, your data goes straight to the recipient without hitting intermediary servers, ensuring maximum privacy and transfer speeds.
No installation or accounts required. Just open and share.
